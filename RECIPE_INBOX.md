# Sundō Recipe Inbox

A private working collection of recipes Cynthia sends for possible future meal-plan weeks. These are **not active app meals** until a week is selected and the plan, shopping list, portions, and prep flow are intentionally updated.

## How to use this file

When building a new Sundō week:

1. Choose recipes from this inbox that fit the week’s protein and ingredient overlap.
2. Confirm any missing portions, nutritional targets, and substitutions with Cynthia.
3. Add only the selected recipes to the live weekly plan, then regenerate the shopping list and prep instructions from that plan.

## 1. Pumpkin Chia Seed Pudding

- **Source:** [Instagram reel by @stephinguyen](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcY9oIaOxzq/)
- **Type:** Prep-ahead breakfast / snack
- **Source quantities:** makes an unspecified number of servings

### Ingredients

- 2 cups milk of choice
- 1 cup pumpkin purée
- 4 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup plain non-fat Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup chia seeds
- Optional topping: diced apple, homemade whipped cream, pumpkin seeds

### Captured method

1. Mix everything except the chia seeds and Greek yogurt.
2. Mix in the Greek yogurt, then the chia seeds.
3. Rest for 5 minutes, then stir again to prevent clumps.
4. Chill and top just before eating.

### Whipped-cream topping

- 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1 tbsp vanilla syrup
- Whip with a hand mixer until stiff.

### Useful overlap

Greek yogurt, chia seeds, milk, maple syrup, pumpkin seeds and apples can overlap with breakfast/snack weeks.

## 3. Honey Garlic Chicken Thighs

- **Source:** [Instagram reel by @stephinguyen](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcY9oIaOxzq/)
- **Type:** Air-fryer protein prep
- **Source quantities:** 2 lb chicken thighs; servings unspecified

### Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp sriracha

### Captured method

1. Mix the chicken with all seasonings and sauces.
2. Air-fry at 375°F / 190°C for 12–15 minutes.

### Useful overlap

Soy sauce, dark soy, oyster sauce, honey, sriracha and chicken thighs work with stir-fries, rice bowls, noodles and the bean salad above.

## 4. High-Protein Beef Bulgogi Bibimbap

- **Source:** [Instagram reel by @ivyskitchn](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Da8EEhlxBEt/)
- **Type:** Korean meal-prep bowl
- **Yield:** 6 bowls
- **Creator’s stated nutrition:** 44 g protein and 6 g fibre per bowl

### Beef bulgogi

- 2 lb 85/15 ground beef
- 8 oz mushrooms, finely chopped
- 1/2 yellow onion, diced
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

### Vegetables

- 3 carrots, julienned
- 2 zucchini, julienned
- 10 oz spinach
- 12 oz bean sprouts
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- Salt and sesame seeds, to taste

### Bibimbap sauce

- 6 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 2–3 tbsp water, to thin

### Plus

- 4 cups uncooked short-grain rice
- 6 eggs
- Kimchi, amount unspecified
- 2 green onions

### Capture note

The public caption supplies the ingredient list and yield, but not detailed cooking steps. Before this goes live in Sundō, preserve the creator’s intended video method or confirm a practical meal-prep method with Cynthia rather than inventing one.

### Useful overlap

Ground beef, mushrooms, carrots, spinach, rice, eggs, spring onions, soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, garlic and honey overlap strongly with Korean and Asian-inspired weekly plans. Gochujang, mirin, kimchi and bean sprouts are the main specialty additions.
